---
title: "Planning Queue Display"
---

Planning Queue Display occupies the top section of Drafting Room showing projects moving through Stages 1-3 of creation.

Each project shows urushi image at early stage (Sketch or Foundation), stage indicator ("Stage 2 of 4 - Scoped"), category seal, and last modified date. Directors can click any project to resume work, pause and return later, or abandon projects no longer relevant.

## Interactive Demo

Experience the Planning Queue in action with our [interactive demo](/static/apps/drafting-room-planning-queue.html).

The demo showcases:
- **Project cards** for each in-progress project with stage indicators
- **Queue health monitoring** with visual warnings for stale projects (14+ days)
- **Resume functionality** to pick up where you left off
- **Abandon with undo** to clean up projects you no longer plan to pursue
- **Marvin's contextual guidance** based on your queue state
- **Empty state handling** when no projects are in progress

Mock data includes projects at different stages (Stage 1-3) across various life categories to demonstrate the full range of Planning Queue features.
