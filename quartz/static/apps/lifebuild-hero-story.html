The Honest Alpha

We're building LifeBuild in public. Rough edges, broken things, occasional catastrophic data loss. Also: you'll shape what this becomes.

On security: Early-stage AI software is an even less secure place for personal information than big AI products—and that's a low bar. If that gives you pause, wait for Public Beta.
